✅ 4 MCP Servers (data access layer)
   - ApplicantDB (port 8001): Applicant profiles, employment, credit history
   - RiskRulesDB (port 8002): Risk thresholds, classification rules
   - DecisionSynthesis (port 8003): Decision policy, risk scoring
   - NotificationSystem (port 8004): Notifications, audit logging

✅ 5 AI Agents (decision logic)
   - ApplicantProfileAgent: Analyzes profile & employment
   - FinancialRiskAgent: Calculates DTI, LTI, detects anomalies
   - LoanDecisionAgent: Synthesizes decision with reasoning
   - ComplianceAgent: Sends notifications, logs audit trail
   - BaseAgent: Infrastructure (Claude integration, logging)

✅ LangGraph Orchestration (workflow)
   - Validates input
   - Runs agents in sequence
   - Routes conditionally (anomalies → manual review)
   - Handles errors gracefully
   - Tracks everything with trace IDs

✅ SQLite Database
   - Applicants, employment history, credit history
   - Risk rules & thresholds
   - Immutable audit logs
   - Cases & notifications

A: Make sure FastAPI and MCP servers started first, wait 2 seconds

Q: No decision result?
